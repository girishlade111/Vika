(01) Included

What you get, every time.

  • Bookkeeping in Xero, QuickBooks or Zoho Books
  • Bank and card reconciliations every week
  • Payroll, with WPS files for UAE teams
  • Management accounts by the tenth working day
  • A cash flow forecast, rolled forward monthly
  • A named accountant who knows your business

(02) How it runs

Four steps, then a routine.

  1. Step 01

    Week one

    We map your accounts, connect your bank feeds and bring last year’s balances across.

  2. Step 02

    Every month

    Receipts in, reconciliations done, payroll run. You approve, we post.

  3. Step 03

    By the tenth

    Management accounts arrive with a one page note on what moved and why.

  4. Step 04

    Every quarter

    A review call with your accountant and the numbers that matter next quarter.
