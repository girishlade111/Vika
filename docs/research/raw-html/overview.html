The Vika system

Every page, every figure, built for firms that get audited.

We build websites for finance firms and kept rebuilding the same parts: fee tables, filing calendars, partner profiles. Vika is that work as a template, with every class and variable named for a reason.

Three homes, one system

Classic, Cinematic and Statement homes, with Studio and Contact in three layouts too. Every section sits on the same Client First classes and variables, so parts move between layouts cleanly.

UAE or UK pricing, no code

Fees switch between AED and GBP, monthly and yearly, with CMS fields and native interactions. Change a price in the CMS and every view updates.

Seven CMS collections

Services, people, insights, case studies, plans, deadlines and questions. Filing deadlines roll forward on their own and featured case studies feed every home.
